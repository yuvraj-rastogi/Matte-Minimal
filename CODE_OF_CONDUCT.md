# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Matte Minimal project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
* Providing constructive, respectful feedback on theme contrast, colors, and bugs.
* Submitting well-documented issue reports and pull requests.
* Respecting the project's license, attribution requirements, and upstream creators.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
* Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or derogatory comments in Issues or Pull Requests.
* Publishing private information of others without explicit permission.
* Plagiarizing or submitting unlicensed third-party work in Pull Requests.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and to ban contributors for abusive or inappropriate behavior.
